# STAR response

**Question:** Tell me about a time you faced a challenge while working on a project or with a team. What did you do, and what was the result?

## Situation
 Last year, I was working on a group project for my practicum class. We divided the work among team members, with some focusing on UI/UX, others on frontend development, and others on backend development. However, because everyone was working on different parts, we sometimes struggled to clearly explain our overall progress when our professor asked for updates.


## Task
I wanted to find a way to improve our communication and make sure everyone understood what the rest of the team was working on.


## Action
I suggested that we start having weekly team meetings where everyone could share what they had accomplished, what they were currently working on, and any challenges they were facing. This helped us stay updated on each other's progress and work together to solve problems.


## Result
As a result, our team became much more organized and communicated better. We were able to give our professor clearer updates and felt more prepared and united when presenting our final project.
